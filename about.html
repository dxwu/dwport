About

Hello. I'm David, a software engineer who spends most of my off-hours with my hands in clay, behind a film camera, or at a piano. I like objects that show how they were made, and I try to keep this site the same way: plain files, no frameworks, edited by hand.

Ceramics is where I've spent the most time recently: wheel-thrown functional ware, mostly stoneware, mostly glazed in the studio's ash and tenmoku glazes. Sculpture and drawing come in bursts. The film photos are whatever a 35mm point-and-shoot happened to be loaded with.

The writing section is essays and the occasional short story. CS projects are side things I've built. Piano is the hobby I'm worst at and love the most. Goodreads is exactly what it sounds like.
